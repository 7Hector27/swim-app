# 025 — Golf score age-group bands use standard swim-meet age groups

**Date**: 2026-10-16  
**Status**: Decided — confirm with Jason

## Decision

Age-group bands for golf score trends use the same age-group buckets as the leaderboard and motivational standards: 6-under, 7-8, 9-10, 11-12, 13-14, 15-18.

## Considered alternative

A ±1 year birthdate window (e.g., for an 11-year-old: swimmers born within one calendar year either side). This would give a tighter peer group.

## Why standard age groups win

1. **Consistency** — the same buckets used for meet seeding, motivational standards, and the public leaderboard. Coaches and swimmers already understand them.
2. **Sample size** — the standard groups accumulate scores faster, making the 10-swimmer threshold for showing bands much easier to reach in small clubs.
3. **Jason's rubric grading** — the descriptors and rubric grades are calibrated per age group, making within-group comparisons meaningful.

## Consequence

Bands will not appear until 10 swimmers in the same age group have a golf score for a given stroke and category. Until then the card shows "Your trend only."
