# Decisions – Screens & Skills (Oct 14, 2026)

## Skill list changes

- Underwater dolphin kick: three separate grades: free, fly, back (see answer 4).
- Rotation: rated per stroke (freestyle, backstroke). No shared body rotation grade.
- Horizontal alignment and hip position: rated per stroke. Backstroke "Body alignment" is backstroke's alignment skill.
- Swimmer-level skills (not per stroke): buoyancy & balance, core stability, static streamline, finger spacing, sculling / feel for the water, exhalation, breath control.
- Rename "Breathing timing" to "Breath timing" everywhere.
- Rename "Stroke-breath coordination" to "Stroke integrity on breath."
- Breath timing = when the breath happens in the stroke cycle.
- Stroke integrity on breath = whether the stroke stays intact during the breath (lead arm, kick, rhythm, hips).
- Freestyle breath timing and stroke integrity on breath: rated left and right separately.
- Breaststroke and fly: one breath timing and one stroke integrity on breath each.
- Backstroke: no breath timing or stroke integrity on breath; rhythmic breathing only.
- Rhythmic breathing: free, fly, back.
- Bilateral breathing: calculated, not rated. Equals the lower of the left-side and right-side freestyle grades. Left and right grades still show underneath.
- Remove freestyle "Breathing mechanics."
- Remove breaststroke "Breathing" (covered by breaststroke breath timing). Breaststroke breath timing has the "Not legal" option: the head must break the surface every stroke cycle.
- Breathing pattern (every stroke, every 2, every 3, etc.) is recorded on fly and free ratings. Each swimmer's profile has a coach-set recommended breathing pattern.
- Streamline (in motion): one shared skill, shown after every start and every push-off; same grade everywhere. Static streamline stays swimmer-level. Push-off stays separate (grades the push itself).
- Backstroke start: 5 components: reaction, push and drive, arch and entry angle, distance, head-arm-hand alignment on entry. ("Streamline off the wall" removed; covered by shared streamline.)
- Add "Streamline off the start" coverage via the shared Streamline skill after track and relay starts.
- Undecided: Block setup / stance as a 7th component for track and relay starts.

## Golf score

- Golf score (time across the pool + stroke count; lower is better) applies to all four strokes. Breaststroke "Propulsion efficiency" becomes the breaststroke golf score.
- Three categories: push-off in the water, dive from the deck, dive from the block. Scores are only compared within the same category.
- Test protocol: one 25, breakout by the flags, consistent stroke counting per stroke, recorded at a set effort (race pace, optionally also easy pace).
- Featured stat on the swimmer profile, shown as the swimmer's own trend with age-group bands for context. Not on the public leaderboard.

## Evaluation screens

Screens follow what the coach is watching. Stroke screens follow race order. Skills that appear on more than one screen are one shared grade: rate it anywhere and every screen updates.

- **Fundamentals**: buoyancy & balance, core stability, static streamline, finger spacing, sculling, exhalation, breath control. Head position (overall) is calculated: display only, no rating buttons.
- **Water Confidence**: submerging, front float, back float, treading water, getting to the wall. Context: Drill and Practice only.

- Start: track start or push-off, then streamline
- Underwaters: underwater dolphin kick (free)
- Breakout
- The swim: head position, horizontal alignment, hip position, rotation, reach before entry, catch, pull, recovery, kick, breath timing (L/R), stroke integrity on breath (L/R), rhythmic breathing, breathing pattern, stroke timing. Bilateral breathing shown as calculated.
- The wall: wall approach, flip turn, push-off, streamline
- Finish
- Numbers: kick rate, tempo, stroke count, golf score

### Backstroke

- Start: backstroke start, then streamline
- Underwaters: underwater dolphin kick (back)
- Breakout
- The swim: head position / stability, body alignment, hip position, rotation, reach before entry, catch, pull, arm recovery, kick, swimming straight, rhythmic breathing, stroke timing
- The wall: wall approach, backstroke flip turn, push-off, streamline
- Finish
- Numbers: kick rate, tempo, stroke count, golf score

### Breaststroke

### Butterfly

### Screen rules

- Section headers (Start, Underwaters, Breakout, The swim, The wall, Finish, Numbers) are collapsible.
- Measured skills (kick rate, tempo, stroke count, golf score, reaction, distance) have a number box plus the 1–5 grade.
- Every repeated skill label includes its stroke.
- Coaching priority goes at the bottom, after rating.
- Fault tag "breaks down 2nd length" is available on underwaters, breakouts and the swim. Consistency is otherwise graded through the level descriptions (levels 4–5).

## Level descriptions drafted

### Exhalation

- Emerging: holds breath underwater; exhales and inhales all after the mouth clears; may come up gasping.
- Developing: exhales underwater when cued, but often holds it, then blows out after the mouth clears.
- Competent: starts exhaling a moment before the mouth breaks the surface on most breaths at practice pace.
- Advanced: correct exhale timing at race pace; ready to inhale the instant the mouth clears.
- Mastery: correct on every breath through the full race distance and under fatigue.
- Fault tags: holds breath, exhales above water, empties too early.

### Freestyle breath timing (left and right, same wording)

- Emerging: lifts the head forward or turns late; stops the stroke to breathe; lead arm drops.
- Developing: turns with the rotation when cued; often late, holding the head out through the recovery.
- Competent: head turns as the breathing-side arm starts its pull and is back to neutral before that hand enters, at practice pace; occasionally late.
- Advanced: correct timing at race pace on most breaths.
- Mastery: correct timing on every breath through the full race distance, under fatigue.

### Freestyle stroke integrity on breath (left and right, same wording)

- Emerging: stroke stops or falls apart on every breath; lead arm drops, kick stops.
- Developing: stroke pauses or the lead arm sinks on most breaths; better when cued.
- Competent: stroke and kick keep going through the breath at practice pace; minor breakdown.
- Advanced: breathing strokes look like non-breathing strokes at race pace.
- Mastery: no breakdown on any breath through the full race distance, under fatigue.
- Fault tags: lead arm drops, kick stops, stroke pauses, hips sink, over-rotates.

### Butterfly breath timing

- Emerging: head lifts late or high; still up when the arms enter the water.
- Developing: head rises with the pull when cued, but is often still up at the catch.
- Competent: head rises with the pull, stays up until mid-recovery and is down before the catch at practice pace; sometimes late.
- Advanced: correct timing at race pace on most breaths.
- Mastery: correct timing on every breath through the full race distance, including under fatigue.

### Butterfly head position (stroke-specific definition)

Head stays in line with the chest press, not leading the wave; chin low and forward on the breath; back down before the arms enter.

### Breaststroke breath timing

- Emerging: head lifts before the pull or after the hands come together, or stays up through the kick.
- Developing: head rises with the pull when cued, but often surfaces after the widest part of the pull or is still up as the arms reach forward.
- Competent: head starts rising as the hands separate, is out by the widest part of the pull, and is back under as the elbows open into the reach, at practice pace; occasionally late.
- Advanced: correct timing at race pace on most strokes.
- Mastery: correct timing on every stroke through the full race distance, under fatigue.
- Fault tags: lifts early, lifts late, surfaces late, returns too slow, returns too fast, stays up through the kick.
