# Swimmer Skills Rubric

Source: Jason (Axiom Aquatics), Oct 9, 2026. Sections 1 to 9 are Jason's content, transcribed as written. Section 10 is implementation notes for the catalog build (Hector's decisions, not Jason's).

Every skill is rated on its own, 1 to 5, across eight categories, and each rating feeds a dependency map that shows what's limiting a swimmer and what to teach next.

## Rating scale

| Rating | Label        | Meaning                                                                                               |
| ------ | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (none) | Not assessed | Not yet observed. Never defaults to 1.                                                                |
| 1      | Emerging     | Limited understanding or execution; needs significant guidance, demonstration and frequent correction |
| 2      | Developing   | Understands the fundamentals; executes inconsistently; needs regular feedback                         |
| 3      | Competent    | Executes independently and consistently in normal practice, with some inefficiencies                  |
| 4      | Advanced     | Efficient, repeatable technique at higher speeds and under fatigue; occasional refinement             |
| 5      | Mastery      | Refined across speeds, distances and competitive conditions, with minimal breakdown                   |

## What every rating records

| Field        | Values                                                                        | Why                                                                       |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rating       | Not assessed, 1 to 5                                                          | The technique grade                                                       |
| Context      | Drill, Practice swim, Race                                                    | Separates what a swimmer can do in control from what holds under pressure |
| Capacity     | Distance (yd) or reps held at that rating                                     | Keeps physical conditioning separate from technique                       |
| Measurement  | Kicks/min, strokes/min, stroke count, time, reaction (s), entry distance (ft) | Raw number for measured skills, so grades can be recalculated             |
| Coach + date | Who rated it, when                                                            | Progress history and coach-consistency checks                             |
| Note         | Observation, correction, drill                                                | The coaching record                                                       |

**Technique vs. capacity:** the rating grades how well the skill is done; capacity records how long it holds. A swimmer with textbook fly who falls apart after 25 yd is a 4 or 5 technically, with a 25 yd capacity.

**Leniency rule:** grade against the expectation for the swimmer's age group (6&U, 7-8, 9-10, 11-12, 13-14, 15-18), then adjust up one band if their times sit in a higher motivational standard than their age suggests. A 5 for a 7-year-old and a 5 for a 13-year-old describe different absolute performances.

## 1. Body Position & Balance

The foundation category: almost every stroke skill builds on these.

| Skill                   | Applies to                                 | Type            | Builds on                     |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Buoyancy & balance      | Swimmer                                    | Rubric          | (none)                        |
| Head position (overall) | Swimmer; rollup of the 4 per-stroke grades | Rubric (rollup) | Buoyancy & balance            |
| Horizontal alignment    | Swimmer                                    | Rubric          | Head position                 |
| Hip position            | Swimmer                                    | Rubric          | Head position, Core stability |
| Core stability          | Swimmer                                    | Rubric          | (none)                        |
| Body rotation           | Free, back                                 | Rubric          | Core stability, Alignment     |
| Static streamline       | Swimmer                                    | Rubric          | Core stability                |
| Finger spacing          | Swimmer                                    | Rubric          | (none)                        |

### Descriptors: Head position (applies per stroke, then rolls up)

1. **Emerging:** head lifts or swings to look forward or breathe; hips drop visibly.
2. **Developing:** neutral when cued; drifts up when swimming fast or breathing.
3. **Competent:** neutral spine, eyes in the correct line for the stroke, at practice pace.
4. **Advanced:** stays neutral at race pace and through breaths; minimal bounce.
5. **Mastery:** stays neutral through the full race distance, including breaths, turns and the finish.

## 2. Breathing & Coordination

| Skill                   | Applies to                         | Type                    | Builds on                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Exhalation              | Swimmer                            | Rubric                  | (none)                                                      |
| Breath control          | Swimmer; graded per event distance | Rubric                  | Exhalation                                                  |
| Rhythmic breathing      | Free, fly, back                    | Rubric                  | Exhalation, Head position                                   |
| Bilateral breathing     | Free                               | Rollup (calculated)     | Calculated as the weaker of left/right breathing timing (free) grades |
| Breathing timing        | Each stroke                        | Rubric                  | Head position, Stroke timing                                |
| Stroke hold on breath   | Each stroke (free, breast, fly)    | Rubric                  | Breathing timing, (free: free-rotation; others: none)       |

### Breath control (by event distance)

Graded against the event length being trained, so one swimmer can be a 5 at 25 yd and a 2 at 50 yd.

| Age group | 25 yd no breath     | 50 yd single breath                                    |
| --------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 5-7       | Mastery expectation | Not expected                                           |
| 8-9       | Mastery expectation | Mastery expectation                                    |
| 10+       | Assumed             | Expected; grade breathing pattern at race pace instead |

### Descriptors: Breath control

1. **Emerging:** breathes every 1-2 strokes; stops or stands to breathe.
2. **Developing:** reaches the flags before the first breath.
3. **Competent:** covers most of the distance on the target breaths; stroke shortens near the wall.
4. **Advanced:** hits the target breaths, but stroke or tempo falls apart at the finish.
5. **Mastery:** hits the target breaths at race tempo, repeatable on normal rest.

## 3. Freestyle

| Skill               | Type             | Unit       | Builds on                                    |
| ------------------- | ---------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Head position       | Rubric           |            | Head position (overall)                      |
| Reach before entry  | Rubric           |            | Body rotation                                |
| Catch               | Rubric           |            | Reach before entry, Finger spacing           |
| Pull                | Rubric           |            | Catch, Core stability                        |
| Recovery            | Rubric           |            | Body rotation                                |
| Kick                | Rubric           |            | Horizontal alignment                         |
| Kick rate           | Measured + grade | kicks/min  | Kick                                         |
| Breathing mechanics | Rubric           |            | Head position, Body rotation, Breath control |
| Stroke timing       | Rubric           |            | Catch, Kick                                  |
| Distance per stroke | Measured + grade | strokes/25 | Catch, Pull, Alignment                       |

### Descriptors: Catch

1. **Emerging:** hand slaps or pushes down; elbow drops first; no feel for the water.
2. **Developing:** fingertips point down when cued; elbow still drops on most strokes.
3. **Competent:** early vertical forearm on most strokes at practice pace; elbow stays above the hand.
4. **Advanced:** consistent high-elbow catch at race pace and on the breathing side.
5. **Mastery:** high-elbow catch holds through the full race distance with no slipping at the finish.

| Skill                     | Type             | Unit      | Builds on                          |
| ------------------------- | ---------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Body alignment            | Rubric           |           | Horizontal alignment, Hip position |
| Head position / stability | Rubric           |           | Head position (overall)            |
| Rotation                  | Rubric           |           | Core stability, Body rotation      |
| Reach before entry        | Rubric           |           | Rotation                           |
| Catch                     | Rubric           |           | Reach before entry, Rotation       |
| Pull                      | Rubric           |           | Catch                              |
| Arm recovery              | Rubric           |           | Rotation                           |
| Kick                      | Rubric           |           | Body alignment                     |
| Kick rate                 | Measured + grade | kicks/min | Kick                               |
| Swimming straight         | Rubric           |           | Head stability, Stroke timing      |
| Stroke timing             | Rubric           |           | Rotation, Catch                    |

### Descriptors: Swimming straight

1. **Emerging:** zig-zags or hits the lane line within a few strokes.
2. **Developing:** drifts but corrects; touches the lane line once or twice per length.
3. **Competent:** holds a line at practice pace; uses flags or ceiling to orient.
4. **Advanced:** stays straight at race pace; occasional drift on the last few strokes.
5. **Mastery:** stays centered at race pace through the full race, including into the wall.

## 5. Breaststroke

| Skill                              | Type             | Unit                                                     | Builds on                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Kick mechanics                     | Rubric           |                                                          | Hip position                  |
| Arm pull                           | Rubric           |                                                          | Finger spacing                |
| Head position                      | Rubric           |                                                          | Head position (overall)       |
| Breathing                          | Rubric           |                                                          | Head position, Arm pull       |
| Timing                             | Rubric           |                                                          | Kick mechanics, Arm pull      |
| Glide                              | Rubric           |                                                          | Static streamline, Timing     |
| Streamline recovery                | Rubric           |                                                          | Static streamline             |
| Stroke rate                        | Measured + grade | strokes/min                                              | Timing                        |
| Propulsion efficiency (golf score) | Score            | time across the pool (s) + stroke count; lower is better | Kick mechanics, Glide, Timing |

Golf-score grade bands are set per age group from the scores recorded over the first few weeks.

### Descriptors: Kick mechanics

1. **Emerging:** scissor or flutter kick; feet not turned out.
2. **Developing:** whip kick shape when cued; feet turn out on some kicks; knees too wide.
3. **Competent:** feet flexed and turned out on every kick at practice pace; knees about hip-width; legal.
4. **Advanced:** narrow, fast whip at race pace; legs finish together into streamline.
5. **Mastery:** powerful, legal kick holds through the full race distance with no shortening at the finish.

## 6. Butterfly

| Skill              | Type             | Unit      | Builds on                          |
| ------------------ | ---------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Body undulation    | Rubric           |           | Core stability, Head position      |
| Dolphin kick       | Rubric           |           | Body undulation                    |
| Kick rate          | Measured + grade | kicks/min | Dolphin kick                       |
| Head position      | Rubric           |           | Head position (overall)            |
| Reach before entry | Rubric           |           | Body undulation                    |
| Catch              | Rubric           |           | Reach before entry, Finger spacing |
| Pull               | Rubric           |           | Catch                              |
| Arm recovery       | Rubric           |           | Body undulation                    |
| Breathing timing   | Rubric           |           | Head position, Breath control      |
| Two-kick rhythm    | Rubric           |           | Dolphin kick, Pull                 |

### Descriptors: Body undulation

1. **Emerging:** bends at the knees or waist; chest stays flat; no wave.
2. **Developing:** chest press visible on some strokes; movement starts at the hips.
3. **Competent:** wave starts at the chest and flows to the feet at practice pace.
4. **Advanced:** tight, controlled undulation at race pace; hips stay near the surface.
5. **Mastery:** undulation drives the stroke through the full race distance, including breathing strokes.

## 7. Starts, Turns & Underwaters

### Underwaters and breakouts

| Skill                   | Applies to                      | Type   | Builds on                         |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------- |
| Underwater dolphin kick | Free and fly (one shared grade) | Rubric | Static streamline, Dolphin kick   |
| Underwater dolphin kick | Back (own grade)                | Rubric | Static streamline, Dolphin kick   |
| Pullout                 | Breast                          | Rubric | Static streamline, Kick mechanics |
| Push-off                | All                             | Rubric | Static streamline                 |
| Breakout                | Each stroke                     | Rubric | Underwater kick, Stroke timing    |

### Descriptors: Underwater dolphin kick (free/fly)

1. **Emerging:** holds streamline only a few yards; arms separate or head lifts; kick small and irregular.
2. **Developing:** recognizable streamline to about the flags; kick present but slow and from the knees.
3. **Competent:** roughly half the pool with locked arms and head between them; moderate tempo.
4. **Advanced:** most or all of the pool with proper form and high kick tempo, but not repeatable lap after lap.
5. **Mastery:** full pool underwater with proper form and high kick tempo, repeatable lap after lap on adequate rest.

Backstroke uses the same descriptors; set its expected distance slightly ahead of free/fly, since back underwaters are faster in practice.

### Starts

Track start and relay start are separate skills, each rated on six components; the start's rating is the average of the components, rounded down.

| Component                        | What a 5 looks like                                                | Type             | Unit |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- | ---- |
| Reaction                         | Leaves on the signal (track) or on a safe, tight exchange (relay)  | Measured + grade | s    |
| Acceleration / force             | Explosive drive off the block, both legs contributing              | Rubric           |      |
| Height                           | Clears the water on a shallow arc; no belly flop or pike-and-drop  | Rubric           |      |
| Distance                         | Enters well out from the wall for size and age                     | Measured + grade | ft   |
| Head-arm-hand alignment on entry | Head tucked between arms, hands stacked, entering through one hole | Rubric           |      |
| Leg and feet position on entry   | Legs together, toes pointed, following through the same hole       | Rubric           |      |

### Turns

Legality first: an illegal turn caps at 2. Then speed into and out of the wall.

| Turn                   | Used in                 | What a 5 looks like                                                            |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Wall approach          | All                     | Adjusts stroke without gliding or chopping; no breath into the wall (free)     |
| Freestyle flip turn    | Free, IM breast to free | Tight tuck; feet plant and push on the back or side into streamline            |
| Backstroke flip turn   | Back                    | Legal single-arm pull onto the stomach, immediate flip, pushes off on the back |
| Breaststroke open turn | Breast                  | Legal two-hand touch, fast elbow-back turn, low push into pullout              |
| Butterfly open turn    | Fly                     | Legal two-hand touch, same fast open turn into dolphin underwaters             |
| Fly to back            | IM                      | Two-hand touch, falls back onto the back off the wall                          |
| Back to breast         | IM                      | Touch on the back, then open, bucket or crossover turn into pullout            |
| Breast to free         | IM                      | Two-hand touch, open turn into free underwaters                                |

## 8. Racing & Performance

These are rated mainly in Race context, or in race-pace practice sets.

| Skill           | Type                             | Unit                        | Builds on                     |
| --------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Tempo           | Measured + grade                 | stroke rate at race pace    | Stroke timing                 |
| Stroke count    | Measured + grade                 | strokes/length at race pace | Distance per stroke           |
| Pacing          | Rubric, from splits              |                             | Tempo, Breath control         |
| Speed endurance | Rubric, from splits and capacity |                             | Pacing                        |
| Finishing       | Rubric                           |                             | Wall approach, Breath control |
| Race strategy   | Rubric                           |                             | Pacing                        |
| Race awareness  | Rubric                           |                             | (none)                        |
| Adaptability    | Rubric                           |                             | Race awareness                |

### Descriptors: Pacing

1. **Emerging:** sprints the start and dies; second half far slower than the first.
2. **Developing:** aware of going out too fast; splits still widely uneven.
3. **Competent:** splits within a reasonable fade for age and event.
4. **Advanced:** even or slightly negative splits in most races.
5. **Mastery:** executes a planned split strategy and adjusts it in-race.

## 9. Dependency map and Jason's notes

When a skill rates low, the app checks the skills it builds on (the "Builds on" columns) and flags the lowest-rated ones as the likely cause.

**Example (freestyle breathing):** a swimmer rated 2 in freestyle breathing with a 2 in body rotation and 4s elsewhere gets body rotation, and behind it core stability, as the coaching priority.

**Jason's suggested data model:**

- Skills: category, name, applies to (swimmer or stroke), type (rubric, measured or score), and the five level descriptors.
- Dependencies: skill to skill, built from the "Builds on" columns.
- Ratings: swimmer, skill, rating or Not assessed, plus context, capacity, measurement, coach, date and note.
- Expectations: the age group and time standard that set the bar for each skill.

One evaluation screen per category lists its skills, with a required coaching-priority line at the bottom. The swimmer profile shows each skill's latest rating, with a trend line from its history.

## 10. Implementation notes (catalog build)

These are build decisions, not part of Jason's rubric. Anything marked "check with Jason" should be listed in the PR summary.

**Skill splitting**

- Skills whose "Applies to" is **Each stroke** become one skill per stroke (free, back, breast, fly): Breathing timing, Stroke-breath coordination, Breakout. Butterfly's own "Breathing timing" row is the fly instance of Breathing timing, not a separate skill.
- Skills with a specific list of strokes stay **one skill** with the list stored in `applies_to`: Body rotation (free, back), Rhythmic breathing (free, fly), Bilateral breathing (free), Pullout (breast), Push-off (all).
- Underwater dolphin kick is **two skills**: one shared for free/fly, one for back.
- Breath control is **one skill**. Grading per event distance is evaluation logic, not separate skills.
- Head position (overall) is type `rollup`, calculated from the four per-stroke head position skills, never rated directly. Rollup method is an open decision.
- Track start and Relay start are type `composite`, each with the six start components as child skills (`parent_skill_id`). Reaction (s) and Distance (ft) are measured. The start's grade is the average of components, rounded down (milestone 3 logic).
- All eight turns are rubric skills in the Starts, Turns & Underwaters category. The illegal-turn cap at 2 is milestone 3 logic, not catalog data.
- Propulsion efficiency (golf score) is type `score`.

**Descriptors**

- Load only the drafted sets: Head position, Breath control, Freestyle catch, Backstroke swimming straight, Breaststroke kick mechanics, Butterfly body undulation, Underwater dolphin kick, Pacing. Don't invent any others.
- Head position descriptors attach to each of the four per-stroke head position skills (they "apply per stroke"). Check with Jason.
- Underwater dolphin kick descriptors attach to both the free/fly and back skills (rubric says back uses the same descriptors).
- Skills without descriptors fall back to the generic rating scale meanings.

**Builds-on resolution**

- Ambiguous names (Head position, Stroke timing, Catch, Alignment, Head stability, Underwater kick, Kick) resolve to the same stroke's skill first, otherwise the Body Position & Balance or Breathing & Coordination version. Examples: "Alignment" means Horizontal alignment; "Head stability" means Backstroke head position / stability; "Breakout builds on Stroke timing" means the same stroke's stroke timing.
- Skills that build on something with no same-stroke version (e.g. Breaststroke breakout and "Stroke timing", since breaststroke uses "Timing") use the closest equivalent. List every resolved link in the PR summary to check with Jason.

**Out of scope for the catalog**

- Expectations / leniency data (age groups, time standards), the breath-control age table, golf-score bands, and kick/stroke-rate target bands. These belong to later milestones once Jason answers the open decisions.
